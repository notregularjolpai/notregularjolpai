## 📌 How I approach a project

1. **Define the question** — What financial or business decision are we trying to support?
2. **Prepare the data** — Inspect sources, clean records, and document assumptions.
3. **Analyze and model** — Choose methods appropriate to the business problem.
4. **Explain the findings** — Turn calculations into understandable insights and limitations.
5. **Make it reproducible** — Share methods, files, and instructions whenever possible.
